Topology State
Prototype Measurement Architecture & Integrity Rules
1. Low-Voltage DC Bus

Engineered exclusively for miniature 12V DC testbed operation (brushless DC fans and LED modules). High-voltage AC mains lines and industrial meters are excluded from the prototype scope.

2. Single Total Shunt Measurement

Gross power draw is captured at the central DC bus supply rail. Per-room electrical isolation is architectural only; individual appliance power breakdowns remain non-interpolated to avoid synthetic estimation.

3. Strict Telemetry Integrity

No synthetic tariff calculations, utility billing rates, or fabricated cost multipliers are generated. All energy analytics represent physical physical-layer Wh accumulations without statistical distortion.
